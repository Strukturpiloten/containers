## Hosting and technical request data

This static website is hosted using **GitHub Pages**, a GitHub service. Requests
to this domain are handled by GitHub and its delivery infrastructure; a company
domain does not mean that requests are served exclusively from Germany.

When your browser requests a page or asset, it transmits technical information
such as your IP address, the requested address, and browser information. Depending
on your browser settings, this can include a referrer. Hosting systems can also
record the request time, response status, and transferred data volume.

These data are used to deliver the website, maintain availability, diagnose
failures, and protect against abuse. Our legal basis for providing a secure,
reliable public catalogue is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests). The
technical request information is necessary to retrieve the website.

GitHub expressly states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for
security purposes, even when visitors are not signed into GitHub. See
[GitHub Pages: data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection).
GitHub also processes data for its own service-security purposes as described in
its [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Retention

We do not operate an additional analytics database or visitor-log collection for
this website. For technical data retained by GitHub, its published retention
criteria are the purpose of processing and applicable legal obligations,
including resolving disputes and enforcing agreements. The GitHub Pages
documentation cited above does not specify a fixed retention period for visitor
IP logs; we therefore do not state a fixed number of days. See GitHub's
[security and retention information](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement#security-and-retention).

Contact enquiries are retained until the enquiry is resolved, unless legal
retention obligations or the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims
require longer storage.

## Cookies, browser storage, and catalogue search

The website's own pages and scripts do not use cookies, local storage, session
storage, analytics, advertising, profiling, or tracking pixels. Styles and scripts
are served from this website; external fonts or third-party embeds are not used.
Ordinary browser caching may store website files to deliver the requested pages.

The image search and family/mode filters operate locally on the catalogue already
loaded in your browser. Search terms are not sent to us or to an external search
provider. The site has no visitor accounts or contact form.

There is currently no consent-based optional tracking for which a cookie banner
would be needed. If optional storage or tracking is introduced, the information
and any required prior consent will be provided before it is used.

in the United States and GitHub B.V. in the Netherlands, and describes processing
in the United States and other countries. This hosting must not be understood as
an exclusively German or EEA-only service.

GitHub describes international-transfer safeguards, including standard
contractual clauses and its participation in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework,

information; the safeguard applicable to a transfer depends on its circumstances.
You can also contact us about processing connected with this website.

We do not sell personal data. Other disclosures occur only where necessary to
operate the service, comply with a legal obligation, or establish, exercise, or
defend legal claims.

## External links and contact enquiries

Links to GitHub repositories, container registries, upstream documentation, and
the company website are ordinary outbound links. Following one is a separate
interaction governed by that service's privacy notice. This does not change the
GitHub Pages hosting connection made when loading this website itself.

If you contact us by email or telephone, we process the information you provide
to handle your enquiry. Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies to contractual or
pre-contractual enquiries; Article 6(1)(f) applies to other enquiries, based on
our legitimate interest in responding to them. You choose whether to contact us;
we may be unable to answer without the information needed for your request.
The company contact page has its own website privacy information.

## Your rights

Subject to the conditions in the GDPR, you may request access, rectification,
erasure, restriction of processing, and data portability. Under Article 21 GDPR,
you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation to processing
based on Article 6(1)(f). Contact us using the details above to exercise these
rights. This website does not make automated decisions about visitors or profile
them.

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority,
particularly in the member state of your habitual residence, workplace, or the
alleged infringement. The authority responsible for Strukturpiloten OHG is the
[State Commissioner for Data Protection of Lower Saxony](https://www.lfd.niedersachsen.de/).
